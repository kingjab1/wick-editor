# Sound library

Sounds for the Sounds tab of the Builtin Library (the + button in the Asset Library).

- **Memes** are meme sounds the site owner added from his own collection. They are not CC0.
- **Whooshes & Hits**, **Drums & Music** and **Robots & Glitch** come from the Sonic Pi sample library (via Adafruit's Sound Samples), all CC0 from Freesound.org.
- **Everyday** comes from Adafruit's Sound Samples ("sounds of adafruit"), CC0, plus a few Sonic Pi samples.
- **Interface** is from Kenney's Interface Sounds (kenney.nl), CC0.
- **Game Blips** are the sounds that ship with the Wick Editor. Each is CC0 from Freesound.org; the credit and link for each one are in `catalog.json`.

A sound pack you bought for your own use usually can't be posted here; check its license first.

To add a sound: put the `.mp3` in a category folder and add an entry to `catalog.json` (`category`, `name`, `file`, `seconds`, `credit`, `license`).
